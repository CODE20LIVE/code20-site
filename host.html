How it works

Simple for you. We handle the rest.

  1. Tell us about your spot

    Fill out the form with your location and what the view looks like. Photos help.

  2. We review the view

    We look at the sightline, access, power and connectivity, and follow up by email.

  3. We set it up

    CODE 20 provides the camera and equipment and gets the stream online.

  4. Your view goes live

    Your location streams 24/7 to viewers around the world. Share it with everyone you know.

What we look for

Views worth watching.

  • Waterfronts, harbors, marinas and beaches
  • Railways and rail yards
  • Overlooks, ridgelines and skylines
  • Airports, landmarks and weather-worthy horizons
  • A safe, elevated mounting spot with a clear sightline
  • Power and internet on site are a plus
Scenic views only. CODE 20 streams ambient, scenic footage. We do not use license plate recognition or facial recognition.
