Delivery

Pick your protocol.

SRT

Secure Reliable Transport. Built-in packet loss recovery and encryption, the preferred contribution protocol for remote sites over cellular and public internet.

RTMP

The universal standard for live ingest. Works with virtually every encoder, decoder and streaming platform in your control room.

HLS

HTTP-based delivery for web players, monitoring walls and OTT workflows. Easy to drop into any browser or media server.
